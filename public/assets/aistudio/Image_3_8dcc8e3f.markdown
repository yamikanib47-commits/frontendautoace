## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an approachable, energetic, and data-transparent automotive ecosystem. It bridges consumer discovery and dealer transactions with confidence, clarity, and enthusiasm. Built around consumer empowerment, it takes the stress out of car buying, leasing, and selling by delivering clear deal metrics, trustworthy reviews, and engaging multimedia.

The visual style merges **Modern Consumer Fintech & Marketplace Minimalism** with high-energy automotive editorial cues:
- **Clean Architectural Canvases**: High-contrast white and cool gray surfaces that make vehicle silhouettes and photography stand out immediately.
- **Electric Utility**: Highlighting key savings, actionable search flows, and EV callouts using vibrant brand blues and indigo accents against deep slate and dark neutral anchors.
- **Approachable Confidence**: Friendly geometric proportions, bold weights, pill-style filtering tags, and structured micro-badges that present complex price matrices in easily digestible formats.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around signature high-luminance primary indigo balanced against stark deep neutrals and clean paper whites:

- **Primary (`#5965fc`)**: The signature brand blue. Used for focal call-to-actions, active tab pills, interactive highlights, search icons, and key conversion points.
- **Secondary (`#6c71b5`)**: An authoritative, deep slate-indigo used for full-bleed dark sections (such as host reviews, advice modules, and footers), prominent headlines, and high-emphasis pricing labels.
- **Tertiary (`#780001`)**: Deep crimson accent, used for EV grant callouts, sustainability tags, Trustpilot badges, and high-value savings metrics.
- **Neutral (`#777681` & `#F5F5F5`)**: High-contrast body typography layered over clean soft slates and crisp neutral card backgrounds.

Apply dark contrast backgrounds selectively across the customer journey to segment editorial, media, or video car review blocks from daytime transactional search grids.

## Typography

The design system relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles, providing a direct match to modern geometric automotive typefaces (such as GT Walsheim) with wide apertures, solid geometry, and friendly curves:

- **Headlines & Display**: Set with extra bold (`800`) weight and slight negative letter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) for a punchy, editorial presence on marketing banners and deal module titles.
- **Vehicle Pricing & Specs**: Always use `800` or `700` weights for numbers, leasing monthly fees, and cash savings banners to ensure instant scanning.
- **Labels & Badges**: Small, compact uppercase or title-case text set at `600` or `700` weight to power review metrics (e.g. `9/10`), EV grant markers, and savings indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system bounded by a max container width of `1280px`:

- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12-column grid with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `3rem` section padding. Deals and news grids display 3 to 4 items per row.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `1rem` gutters. Product carousels transform into 2-column stacked or horizontally scrollable rows with a peek layout.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `0.75rem` (12px) gutters and `1rem` margins. Category navigation pills, vehicle deal cards, and video reviews convert into smooth horizontal snap-scroll rails.

Vertical spacing follows strict multiples of `8px` (`0.5rem`), reserving `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` for high-impact section transitions (e.g. moving from light deal finders to deep-slate editorial video carousels).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows in favor of crisp boundary planes, delicate surface tints, and high-clarity edge borders:

- **Surface Layers**: Content cards sit on a clean base of pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted slate (`#F5F5F5`), rimmed by a crisp 1px neutral stroke (`#E5E7EB` / `#E2E8F0`).
- **Ambient Card Depth**: Default vehicle and video cards rely on a subtle, ultra-soft shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.03)`.
- **Card Hover State**: On interaction, cards smoothly elevate with a transformed Y-axis of `-3px` and an expanded shadow: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Overlays & Controls**: Filter bars, floating search toggles, and modal drawers utilize frosted glass backdrops (`backdrop-blur-md` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`) combined with elevated pill-shaped action buttons.

## Shapes

The geometric form language balances modern digital interfaces with friendly, accessible automotive styling:

- **Cards & Visual Containers**: Utilize `roundedness: 2` (base border radius of `0.75rem` / 12px to `1rem` / 16px). This gives vehicle silhouette thumbnails, deal containers, and video modules soft, finished corners that feel modern.
- **Interactive Badges & Pills**: Filter chips, fuel-type selectors (`EVs`, `Lease`, `Used`), and savings callouts use full pill radii (`9999px`) to invite touch interactions.
- **Input Fields & Search Hubs**: Rounded pill or soft-capsule forms (`9999px` or `0.75rem`) creating a welcoming, friendly, Google-like discovery search bar.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Pill or rounded-lg button filled with brand primary (`#5965fc`), dark charcoal bold text (`#171717`) or pure white, featuring high hover feedback.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: White background with a 1px border (`#D1D5DB`) and bold dark text (`#171717`), shifting to soft primary-tinted background on hover.
- **Round Icon Buttons**: Circular navigation and directional trigger buttons (e.g. carousel next/prev arrows) rendered in pure black `#171717` with white icons or white with a 1px border.

### Filter & Category Chips
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) tags displaying category icons (e.g., Lightning bolt for `EVs`, Car silhouette for `SUVs`, Tag for `Under £20k`).
- Unselected state uses pure white or slate background (`#F5F5F5`) with subtle border; active state switches to brand primary (`#5965fc`) or deep charcoal (`#111827`) with contrasting white text.

### Vehicle Deal Cards
- **Structure**: 
  1. Top title area: Vehicle Make & Model (`headline-sm`), spec subtitle, and high-contrast savings pill badge (`Avg saving £3,742 off RRP`).
  2. Image canvas: Clean lateral photography of the vehicle with neutral background.
  3. Spec & Pricing footer: Dual-price display (Cash purchase price alongside Monthly Lease price) with a black circular right-arrow CTA for quick configuration.

### Review & Score Badges
- **Rating Pills**: High-contrast rounded pill (`#5965fc` or `#780001`) displaying editorial scores (e.g., `8/10`, `9/10`) positioned directly below video thumbnails and test-drive journalism headers.
- **Trustpilot Integration**: Distinct green 5-star logo module (`#00B67A`) featuring live star ratings and clear review counts (`4.3/5 from 85,653 reviews`).

### Search & Discovery Bar
- Prominent elevated capsule containing an intuitive prompt ("Find a car by fuel type or model..."), a fuel-type filter dropdown, and a brand-primary circular search button containing a magnifying glass.

### Accordion / FAQ Modules
- Clean, divider-free white rows with bold label text, collapsible body explanations, and chevron icons that rotate smoothly on expansion.
